# Quality Control

Run this audit privately after drafting and revise once when a critical check fails.

## Decision integrity

- Does the verdict address one explicit primary success objective?
- Are showcase, adoption, community, learning, and commercial optionality kept distinct?
- Did a fatal flaw get averaged away?
- Does confidence reflect evidence quality rather than enthusiasm?
- Is the project worth its build and recurring maintenance opportunity cost?

## Evidence integrity

- Are current alternatives, repository status, licensing, ecosystem, and technical claims researched and cited?
- Are stars, downloads, forks, issues, contributors, and mentions interpreted with their limitations?
- Is production use verified rather than inferred?
- Are facts, inferences, assumptions, unknowns, and contradictions distinguishable?
- Is desk research separated from real user behavior?

## Product and open-source fit

- Does a specific user receive standalone value?
- Is there a concrete reason source availability improves the outcome, control, or collaboration model?
- Does the substitute set include mature open source, commercial products, platforms, internal code, generic tools, and doing nothing?
- Is the wedge a user, workflow, integration, technical, trust, or stewardship advantage rather than a feature list?

## Credibility and adoption

- Does the artifact demonstrate capabilities relevant to the intended audience?
- Are documentation, examples, tests, types, local setup, architecture, and security prioritized according to the evidence they need to produce?
- Is developer experience treated as an adoption and credibility mechanism rather than an automatic moat?
- Can a relevant user understand, install, and reach value without disproportionate friction?
- Were launch, promotion, audience-building, and outreach advice omitted?

## Maintenance and opportunity

- Are compatibility, triage, support, releases, security, governance, and abandonment considered?
- Is a recurring maintenance budget explicit?
- Are future support, consulting, hosting, enterprise, sponsorship, licensing, or career paths labeled as supported or speculative?
- Does dual licensing account for ownership, dependencies, contributions, and community trust before being suggested?

## Validation and output

- Does the test target the riskiest assumption with real user behavior?
- Can it begin without building the full product?
- Are pass, iterate, and kill thresholds explicit and tied to the success contract?
- Does the answer say what not to build or polish yet?
- Is the recommendation actionable without becoming a full roadmap?

If the draft fails three or more checks, reconsider the investigation rather than merely editing the prose.
