# Quality Control

Run this audit privately after drafting. Revise once when any critical check fails.

## Decision integrity

- Does the verdict follow from the seven gates?
- Did a fatal flaw get averaged away or softened?
- Is confidence based on evidence quality rather than enthusiasm?
- Would a rational founder understand why this idea beats or loses to their opportunity cost?

## Evidence integrity

- Are current, niche, legal, pricing, competitor, integration, and market claims researched and cited?
- Are vendor claims described as claims rather than proof of effectiveness?
- Are facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns distinguishable?
- Is contradictory evidence preserved and explained?
- Is desk research clearly separated from real validation?
- Did the analysis avoid TAM, growth rates, search volume, and social engagement as proof of demand?

## Competition and wedge

- Does the substitute set include services, internal labor, generic tools, DIY, and doing nothing?
- For AI, research, advice, content, or idea products, does it include general-purpose AI plus a strong prompt?
- Is the wedge stated in terms of buyer, trigger, workflow, outcome, trust, or responsibility—not only features?
- Is the crowding classification consistent with the evidence?

## Business quality

- Are recurring and non-recurring revenue separated?
- Is recurrence supported by a repeated trigger, monitoring need, portfolio buyer, or repeated usage?
- Are support, onboarding, customization, security, privacy, uptime, and on-call burden considered?
- Are build, domain, and trust advantages treated separately?

## Pivot quality

- Does every pivot repair a named weak or unknown gate?
- Does each pivot change no more than two variables?
- Is at least one version materially simpler when plausible?
- Were cosmetic feature additions and random industry shifts removed?

## Validation quality

- Does the test target the riskiest assumption?
- Are prospects specific and qualified?
- Is the offer outcome-based and deliverable manually?
- Does the test require a meaningful commitment?
- Are pass, iterate, and kill thresholds explicit and behavior-based?
- Can the user begin the test without building production software?

## Output quality

- Is the response within the mode's scope and the 1,200-word default?
- Is there one coherent answer rather than several profile reports?
- Are tables compact and non-repetitive?
- Are missing evidence and kill conditions explicit?
- Is the recommendation actionable tomorrow?

If the draft fails three or more checks, reconsider the investigation rather than merely editing the prose.
